# Pages

- **Desk**, under Open and View: the blotter, positions, a watchlist, a chart
  and an instrument picker, in a docking layout. "Reset layout" puts the
  default layout back.
- **Datasets**, under Manage: each dataset the plugin reads, and whether it
  is entitled to it.

Each read the desk shows is also a read tool on the deployment's MCP
surface, under Open and View as the desk is: `read_blotter` (the blotter,
and what changed since a previous read), `read_positions`, `read_instrument`
(the picker), `read_latest_price` (the watchlist), and `read_bar_lengths` and
`read_bars` (the chart). Each answers what its panel shows, cut to the same
accounts, and is refused as its panel is. A price is answered as the lake
records it, as text.

The desk works at widths from 375 px with no sideways scroll.
